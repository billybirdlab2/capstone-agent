#### 1. Assessing Vision-Language Models for Early Prediction of Unsafe Situations in Robotics
Muhammad Farooq et al. · 2026-11-18 · http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24146v1 · track 3 (robotic safety)

- **Contribution (abstract):** Empirical evaluation of whether general-purpose VLMs can predict robot failures before an unsafe situation occurs.
- **Approach (abstract):** Qwen, InternVL and LLaVA-OneVision classify situations as safe / potentially unsafe / unsafe from video and odometry in three case studies (HuRoN, Pal Robotics, CrowdBot), with uncertainty quantified by several metrics. Reported: Qwen strongest on overall classification and prediction reliability; InternVL stronger early-warning capability in several configurations; effect of robot-state and temporal history is model- and objective-dependent.
- **Limitations (from abstract):** Three case studies, mostly social/mobile navigation rather than industrial settings; no quantitative scores in the abstract; no single best configuration — choice depends on whether accuracy, calibration or warning lead time is prioritized.
- **Relevance (interpretation):** Closest baseline to the capstone: a pure-VLM safety classifier. Experiment: compare these end-to-end VLM safe/unsafe judgments against a pipeline of VLM-grounded relational predicates + ILP-learned rules on the same clips, measuring accuracy, calibration and lead time.

#### 2. EmboEagle: An Efficient Agentic Embodied Framework for Long-horizon Robotic Manipulation
Fan Xu et al. · 2026-11-23 · http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31173v1 · tracks 1, 5

- **Contribution (abstract):** Closed-loop agentic framework combining subtask-conditioned World-Action-Model control, latent-space adaptive supervision, and memory-guided geometric recovery.
- **Approach (abstract):** Two-stage flow matching for sequential WAMs; a lightweight routing gate distilled from teacher intervention decisions decides when to invoke a VLM; the VLM separates diagnostic status from discrete routing actions, executes parameterized recovery primitives grounded in structured memory, then performs mandatory post-repair verification. Reported: 91.3% / 90.6% average success on RMBench / LIBERO-Mem (+8.0 / +9.8 pp over strongest baselines); adaptive gating cuts VLM calls by 74.2% vs chunkwise monitoring (91.3% vs 93.7% success).
- **Limitations (from abstract):** Gating trades a small success drop (93.7% → 91.3%) for fewer VLM calls; real-world gains reported only qualitatively against one baseline (VoLoAgent); recovery is limited to a predefined primitive set.
- **Relevance (interpretation):** Directly mirrors the planned agentic contribution — diagnose failure, pick a bounded corrective action, verify afterwards. Its "diagnosis ≠ action" separation and bounded primitive set are a design template; the recovery choice could instead be conditioned on violated ILP safety rules.

#### 3. Trustworthy agentic lunar robotics: safety-constrained contingent planning with a foundation-model planner
Bingqi Huang et al. · 2026-11-23 · http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30179v1 · track 3

- **Contribution (abstract):** Asymmetric knowledge-level replanning (AKR), treating a foundation-model planner as an untrusted black box under safety constraints with irreversible actions.
- **Approach (abstract):** Reversible actions run on the planner's assumptions; irreversible actions run only after a minimum-cost sensing cover verifies every precondition. Claims (under sound measurements) that no irreversible action executes on a false precondition, with bounded sensing cost and verified-goal-or-certified-hold termination. Reported: no hazardous action in 21,000 Monte Carlo trials or 58 full-stack runs (FF-Replan rule: one in 7% of trials); median run 1.10× fastest (vs 1.50× K-replanner); 20 of 21 contingency runs completed.
- **Limitations (from abstract):** Guarantees hinge on "sound measurements"; domain is lunar-base logistics; three-valued evidence and sensing costs must be modeled explicitly.
- **Relevance (interpretation):** Strong conceptual fit for "independently verified safety rules": a VLM proposes, a verifier gates irreversible actions on checked preconditions. The reversible/irreversible split is a concrete way to bound corrective actions in an industrial setting.

#### 4. Object-Centric Differentiable Logic Networks for Abstract Visual Reasoning (Slot-Dwn)
Hanne Say Eren et al. · 2026-11-23 · http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30736v1 · track 2 (neuro-symbolic)

- **Contribution (abstract):** Neuro-symbolic architecture unifying unsupervised object-centric perception with logic-based reasoning in Boolean space.
- **Approach (abstract):** Multi-Head Differentiable Weightless Network using look-up tables, with a LUT-based relational bottleneck and sparse input routing over object slots. Reported competitive performance under extreme out-of-distribution regimes with up to 52% fewer trainable parameters and faster convergence, on rule generalization, sample-efficient anomaly detection and multi-relational reasoning tasks.
- **Limitations (from abstract):** Abstract visual reasoning benchmarks only (no real scenes); learned LUTs may be less human-readable than explicit clauses; "competitive" rather than superior.
- **Relevance (interpretation):** An alternative to VLM-produced predicates: slots → Boolean relations. Experiment: compare rule generalization from LUT-based relational learning vs ILP over VLM-grounded predicates on a small synthetic hazard dataset; the anomaly-detection framing maps naturally to unsafe-configuration detection.

#### 5. Temporal Logic Tensor Networks: Foundations of Temporal Reasoning in Neural Networks
Edjard Mota et al. · 2026-11-20 · http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28277v1 · track 2

- **Contribution (abstract):** tLTN, a differentiable neuro-symbolic framework with a multi-sorted logic where events and states are interdependent.
- **Approach (abstract):** Extends Interval LTN; state predicates are fuzzy truth-value paths over time points; events have learnable trapezoidal fuzzy durations and differentiable Allen-style relations; interval and point-wise layers constrain each other (e.g., Until/Since evaluated only within event-defined spans).
- **Limitations (from abstract):** Only controlled synthetic examples; no real video benchmarks or quantitative results in the abstract.
- **Relevance (interpretation):** Industrial safety rules are often temporal ("no entry *until* the machine has stopped"). tLTN offers a formalism for temporal predicates over video; experiment: express a few such rules in tLTN and check whether they are satisfiable from VLM-grounded per-frame predicates.

#### 6. Seeking in Space: Benchmarking Purposeful Active Exploration of Vision-Language Models for Spatial Reasoning
Xiwei Liu et al. · 2026-11-23 · http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31215v1 · track 1 (VLM/grounding)

- **Contribution (abstract):** ActSpace-Bench — 994 questions, 234 exploration episodes, 72 real-scanned indoor scenes — where a VLM must gather evidence before answering.
- **Approach (abstract):** Protocol fixes each model as reasoner and compares its exploration to a same-budget random walk and an oracle route; masked-evidence questions test abstention; >95% of sampled questions confirmed correct by human verification. Across 23 VLMs, strongest models seek evidence purposefully but lose much of it to limited memory; weaker/specialized ones explore no better than random.
- **Limitations (from abstract):** Indoor domestic scenes; question-answering rather than action or safety outcomes.
- **Relevance (interpretation):** Two hooks: (a) abstention when evidence is missing is exactly what a safety-rule checker needs; (b) "evidence seeking" fits the planned evidence-based diagnosis step. Experiment: test whether the VLM in the capstone pipeline abstains on predicates whose visual evidence is occluded.

#### 7. JEPA-Shield: Safe Diffusion Planning with Latent World Models via Conformal Shielding
Taekyung Kim et al. · 2026-11-22 · http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29609v1 · track 4 (robot learning/control)

- **Contribution (abstract):** Connects a frozen action-conditioned JEPA world model to task-space safety enforcement for sampling-based MPC.
- **Approach (abstract):** Downstream models predict relative motion from imagined latent rollouts and decode unsafe geometry from the image; split conformal prediction gives multi-horizon uncertainty tubes; a backup-based shield screens candidate trajectories and final proposals. Claims probabilistic constraint satisfaction at action-block boundaries under exchangeability and initial-backup assumptions. Reported success 84.4% / 82.3% / 99.0% on the VParkBench tasks with no observed constraint violations.
- **Limitations (from abstract):** Guarantee is probabilistic, conditional on exchangeability, and applies to a plan executed without replanning; evaluated on parking tasks (car, tractor-trailer), not manipulation.
- **Relevance (interpretation):** A calibrated shield is a natural enforcement layer downstream of learned safety rules. Experiment: use conformal calibration to bound the error of VLM-grounded predicates before they feed a rule checker.

#### Synthesis (interpretation)
- **Propose-then-verify is the week's dominant pattern.** AKR (#3), FLINT (#8) and EmboEagle's post-repair verification (#2) all treat the neural/foundation model as an untrusted proposer and gate consequential decisions on an independent check. This is the architecture the capstone already assumes; AKR's reversible/irreversible split and FLINT's evidence-citing certificates are two concrete mechanisms worth borrowing.
- **A ready-made baseline for the capstone.** Farooq et al. (#1) give a pure-VLM safety classifier with uncertainty; pairing it with a predicate + ILP pipeline on the same data is the most direct comparison experiment this week.
- **Representing rules: Boolean, temporal, calibrated.** Slot-Dwn (#4) learns relational rules as Boolean LUTs, tLTN (#5) adds temporal operators, and JEPA-Shield (#7) shows how conformal calibration turns noisy predictions into enforceable bounds. Together they suggest an ILP rule set with temporal predicates whose input predicates carry conformal error bounds.
- **Evidence and abstention.** ActSpace-Bench (#6) and EmboEagle (#2) both stress that VLMs lose or ignore evidence; the safety pipeline should measure abstention on occluded predicates rather than force a verdict.
